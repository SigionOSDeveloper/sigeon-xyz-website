About SigeonOS

SigeonOS pretty much started as an imaginary operating system until I (SigeonOSDeveloper) got inspired and decided to develop the first, built from scratch, operating system of SigeonOS.

Ever since then, I've gotten love from the community and decided to continue on SigeonOS, began from a Linux kernel, to SigeonOS: Classic, till now the current version of SigeonOS, SigeonOS: Breadcrumb Release.

The project is being developed to be open source and in the open. Issues, patches, and questions are all welcome.
